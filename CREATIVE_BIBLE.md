# DINKLY Creative Bible

## Mission

DINKLY celebrates the tiny, ordinary moments that become meaningful when shared with someone you love.

The core emotional idea is:

> Ordinary life is better together.

DINKLY is not built around dramatic romance. It is built around comfort, companionship, warmth, routine, belonging, home, playfulness, small acts of care, healthy relationships, and quiet affection.

The desired audience response is:

> That reminds me of us.

## What DINKLY is

DINKLY is an original character IP. Comics are its storytelling engine, not its final boundary. Every creative decision should strengthen a world that can naturally extend into plushies, card games, books, stickers, stationery, home goods, animation, collectibles, collaborations, and brand partnerships.

## Emotional North Star

Every approved story should express at least one primary emotion:

- Comfort
- Belonging
- Togetherness
- Routine
- Home
- Peace
- Affection
- Support
- Playfulness
- Gratitude

Emotion should be visible before text is read. Dialogue may add nuance, but it must not carry the entire scene.

## Relationship philosophy

DINKLY is companionship rather than aspirational dating content. The relationship is achievable, healthy, comfortable, and supportive.

Use:

- Quiet presence
- Familiar routines
- Thoughtful gestures
- Comfortable physical closeness
- Shared boredom
- Low-stakes playfulness
- Mutual care

Avoid:

- Arguments
- Jealousy
- Breakups
- Manipulation
- Status games
- Possessiveness
- Sexualized humor
- Luxury as proof of love
- Conflict created only to produce a punchline

## Story engine

The primary engine is:

`ORDINARY ALONE` → `WARMER TOGETHER`

The activity changes very little. The emotional meaning changes because it is shared.

The left side may feel neutral, bored, or gently sad, but never devastated. The right side may feel warm and joyful, but never unrealistically perfect.

## Dinko and Dinka

### Dinko

Dinko is curious, quiet, thoughtful, slightly awkward, and optimistic. His life does not become better because the setting becomes more impressive. It becomes warmer because Dinka is there.

### Dinka

Dinka is playful, warm, gentle, encouraging, and kind. She brings warmth into ordinary moments without becoming frantic, loud, or dramatic.

Neither character is a flawless ideal. Their appeal comes from recognizable comfort and sincere companionship.

## Brand personality

DINKLY is:

- Optimistic
- Gentle
- Playful
- Soft
- Wholesome
- Thoughtful
- Minimal
- Modern

DINKLY is never loud, chaotic, edgy, cruel, sarcastic, cynical, or trend-dependent.

## Creative decision filter

Before approving a concept, ask:

1. Is the ordinary moment instantly recognizable?
2. Is the emotional change visible without explanation?
3. Does togetherness—not a better object or setting—create the warmth?
4. Is the relationship healthy and achievable?
5. Does the idea feel unmistakably DINKLY?
6. Can it become a lasting licensed image rather than a disposable meme?

If any answer is no, improve the concept before prompting.

## Prohibited subject matter

DINKLY does not use politics, violence, swearing, adult jokes, arguments, jealousy, breakups, luxury flexing, money, status, materialism, or influencer culture as story engines.

## Licensing lens

Strong DINKLY artwork should retain meaning on a greeting card, sticker, mug, calendar, book page, plush concept, animation frame, or collectible. Avoid scene logic that only works because of a transient caption or platform trend.

## Long-term test

Every story, product, and partnership must answer:

> Does this make ordinary moments feel a little more special?

If it depends on shock value, complexity, or borrowed cultural relevance, it is probably not on-brand.

# Laundry

## Approval status

Approved creative example. No performance metrics attached.

## Format

`X / X WITH YOU`.

## Title pair

```text
LAUNDRY
LAUNDRY WITH YOU
```

## Emotional insight

A repetitive chore becomes parallel play and a small act of teamwork.

## Left scene

Dinko stands on the floor beside one low laundry basket and a small folded stack, holding one flat towel close to his body with nub arms.

## Right scene

Dinka and Dinko stand together beside the same basket. Each performs one simple task: Dinka passes one towel; Dinko adds it to the stack.

## Direction

- Background: warm sand
- Accent: dusty blue basket
- Camera: medium straight-on
- Props: basket, towel, folded stack, low shelf

## Execution risks

- Human hands or long arms during passing
- Too many clothing items and colors
- Characters standing inside the basket
- Stack obscuring nub feet

## Reusable lesson

Divide the teamwork into one clear action per character and keep the laundry visually abstract.

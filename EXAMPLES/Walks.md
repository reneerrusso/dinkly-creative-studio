# Walks

## Approval status

Approved creative example. No performance metrics attached.

## Format

`X / X WITH YOU`.

## Title pair

```text
WALKS
WALKS WITH YOU
```

## Emotional insight

The route stays the same, but side-by-side presence turns movement into shared time.

## Left scene

Dinko moves along a simple curved path with a neutral expression. Use only nub feet attached to the body; imply movement with a slight forward lean rather than legs.

## Right scene

Dinka and Dinko move side by side on the same path, bodies close and warmly smiling. Keep the path, tree silhouette, and camera identical.

## Direction

- Background: powder blue
- Accent: muted coral path-edge flowers
- Camera: profile medium-wide
- Props: one curved path, one rounded tree, two small flower shapes

## Execution risks

- Long walking legs
- One character larger because of depth
- Added landscape detail
- Characters floating above the path

## Reusable lesson

Movement can be communicated through lean and spacing; complex anatomy is unnecessary.

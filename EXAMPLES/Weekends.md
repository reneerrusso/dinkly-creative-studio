# Weekends

## Approval status

Approved creative example. No performance metrics attached.

## Format

`X / X WITH YOU`.

## Title pair

```text
WEEKENDS
WEEKENDS WITH YOU
```

## Emotional insight

Unstructured time becomes meaningful without needing a special plan.

## Left scene

Dinko sits neutrally on a floor cushion beside one closed book and one mug. The room remains intentionally sparse.

## Right scene

Dinka and Dinko sit on two touching floor cushions in the same position, each doing a quiet activity while their bodies lean together.

## Direction

- Background: pistachio
- Accent: dusty rose cushions
- Camera: medium-wide straight-on
- Props: cushions, book, two mugs, simple rug

## Execution risks

- Adding a complex weekend activity
- Different room arrangement between panels
- Furniture dwarfing the characters
- Left panel appearing lonely rather than ordinary

## Reusable lesson

Doing nothing is a valid DINKLY activity when body language makes companionship visible.

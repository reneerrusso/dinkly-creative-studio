# Shopping

## Approval status

Approved creative example. No performance metrics attached.

## Format

`X / X WITH YOU`.

## Title pair

```text
SHOPPING
SHOPPING WITH YOU
```

## Emotional insight

A practical errand becomes playful shared decision-making.

## Left scene

Dinko stands on the floor beside a small rounded shopping cart, looking neutrally at a short shelf with three simple groceries.

## Right scene

Dinka and Dinko stand together on the floor beside the same cart and shelf. Dinka points with a nub arm toward one item while Dinko smiles.

## Direction

- Background: warm cream
- Accent: muted sage cart
- Camera: medium three-quarter view
- Props: cart, low shelf, three grocery shapes

## Execution risks

- Characters inside the cart
- Human pointing arm or finger
- Oversized cart
- Busy supermarket aisle

## Reusable lesson

Keep the cart secondary and the characters grounded beside it; the shared choice is the story.

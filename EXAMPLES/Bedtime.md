# Bedtime

## Approval status

Approved creative example. No performance metrics attached.

## Format

Split comparison or close-up.

## Title pair

```text
BEDTIME
BEDTIME WITH YOU
```

## Emotional insight

The end of the day feels safer when quiet presence is nearby.

## Left scene

Dinko rests on a visible rounded bed surface under one simple blanket, awake and neutral. His two hair tufts remain visible above the blanket.

## Right scene

Dinka and Dinko rest side by side on the same bed surface under the same blanket. Their equal-size bodies touch gently; both look calm and sleepy.

## Direction

- Background: dusty blue
- Accent: warm cream blanket
- Camera: side-profile medium
- Props: bed, blanket, one rounded bedside table, one lamp

## Execution risks

- Human legs under the blanket
- Bed or blanket obscuring bow and hair tufts
- One character becoming smaller
- Realistic bedroom lighting

## Reusable lesson

Sleepy warmth comes from body proximity and calm expressions, not a detailed bedroom.

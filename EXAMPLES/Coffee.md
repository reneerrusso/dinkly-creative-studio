# Coffee

## Approval status

Approved creative example. No performance metrics attached.

## Format

`X / X WITH YOU` using `PROMPT_TEMPLATES/XWithYou.md`.

## Title pair

```text
COFFEE
COFFEE WITH YOU
```

## Emotional insight

Coffee is already pleasant. Sharing the quiet first cup turns it into companionship without making the setting more impressive.

## Left scene

Dinko sits properly on one rounded café chair at a small table, cradling one cream mug. He looks neutral and slightly bored. A simple counter and compact coffee machine sit behind him.

## Right scene

Dinka and Dinko sit on separate matching chair seats at the same table. Their bodies gently touch, and each cradles a matching mug. The same counter and coffee machine remain in place.

## Direction

- Background: one continuous dusty peach
- Accent: muted sage chairs
- Camera: medium, straight-on
- Props: table, chairs, mugs, counter, coffee machine
- Text: bottom, Bubblebody Neue Regular, no quotation marks or added punctuation

## Execution risks

- Characters sitting on the table instead of chair seats
- Oversized mugs
- Dinko changing scale between panels
- Different background shades
- Too much café detail

## Reusable lesson

Repeat the environment closely so togetherness—not a better café—creates the emotional change.

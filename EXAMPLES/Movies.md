# Movies

## Approval status

Approved creative example. No performance metrics attached.

## Format

`X / X WITH YOU`.

## Title pair

```text
MOVIES
MOVIES WITH YOU
```

## Emotional insight

The screen content is secondary; the real comfort is having someone beside you.

## Left scene

Dinko sits properly on one visible sofa cushion facing a simple television silhouette. He holds one small snack bowl and looks neutral.

## Right scene

Dinka and Dinko sit on separate visible sofa cushions, bodies gently touching, facing the same television. They share one snack bowl placed between them.

## Direction

- Background: soft lavender
- Accent: muted coral snack bowl
- Camera: over-the-shoulder wide
- Props: sofa, television, low console, snack bowl, blanket

## Execution risks

- Sofa hiding nub feet and body silhouettes
- Human arms reaching across the bowl
- Detailed screen content competing with characters
- Too many snacks and colors

## Reusable lesson

Use the screen as a quiet context cue; closeness and shared props carry the emotion.

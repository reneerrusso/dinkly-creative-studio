# Mornings

## Approval status

Approved creative example. No performance metrics attached.

## Format

`X / X WITH YOU`.

## Title pair

```text
MORNINGS
MORNINGS WITH YOU
```

## Emotional insight

The first minutes of the day feel gentler when the routine is shared.

## Left scene

Dinko stands neutrally beside a low breakfast counter with one mug and a small plate. Keep him on the floor, not on the counter.

## Right scene

Dinka and Dinko stand together on the floor beside the same counter. Dinka pours coffee while Dinko steadies the mug close to his body; each has one action.

## Direction

- Background: butter yellow with enough contrast against the characters
- Accent: muted coral appliance
- Camera: medium three-quarter view
- Props: low counter, compact coffee maker, mug, plate, chair

## Execution risks

- Characters standing on the counter
- Long arms or fingers during pouring
- Oversized mug or appliance
- Low contrast between yellow characters and background

## Reusable lesson

A small act of care can replace the static right-panel pose when the action remains simple and spatially precise.

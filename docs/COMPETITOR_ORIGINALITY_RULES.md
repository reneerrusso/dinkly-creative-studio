# Competitor Originality Rules

## Principle

Public-post research is used to discover audience behavior and reusable storytelling principles, never to reproduce another creator’s expression. DINKLY must remain recognizably DINKLY in character design, emotional worldview, copy, composition, props, color system, and execution.

## What can transfer

Abstract patterns may inform a test when evidence supports them. Examples include short contrast titles, a familiar routine, a readable emotional shift, low text load, one clear interaction, seasonal timing, or a compact prop story.

The system records the source pattern separately from the reusable principle. A principle should be broad enough that many original scenes could express it.

## What must not transfer

Do not copy or closely paraphrase another creator’s caption, dialogue, joke, title pair, character pose, camera arrangement, distinctive prop grouping, setting design, color composition, visual gag, sequence, branded treatment, audio edit, or recognizable art style.

Do not use a public post as a character or style reference in a production prompt. Official DINKLY model sheets are the only character identity source.

## Direction requirements

Every competitor-informed DINKLY direction must include:

- the evidence-backed signal;
- the abstract source pattern;
- a reusable principle;
- an explicit must-not-copy boundary;
- a DINKLY emotional angle grounded in ordinary life together;
- new left and right scenes;
- a shared DINKLY setting and purposeful props;
- approved pastel and restrained accent choices;
- execution risks;
- why the result is original;
- why someone may share it, framed as a hypothesis.

## Distance test

Before handoff, ask:

1. Would the concept still make sense if the source post disappeared?
2. Is the activity or emotional angle new to DINKLY’s library?
3. Are scene layout and props independently chosen?
4. Is the text original and on-brand?
5. Does the direction express “ordinary life is better together” rather than the source creator’s identity?
6. Could a reviewer recognize the source from this execution alone?

If the last answer is yes, increase creative distance or reject the direction.

## Prompt Builder handoff

Handoff creates a normal structured DINKLY concept and opens the existing Prompt Builder. The prompt should cite the DINKLY character references and bibles, not the competitor post. Only the abstract creative insight and original scene plan may carry forward.

## Brand safety

Visible third-party brands in public posts are not automatically opportunities. A direction may name a product category when natural, but a real brand integration requires separate approval, product references, usage rights, and the Brand Partnerships workflow.

## Review record

Keep rejected directions instead of silently deleting evidence of the decision. Mark why the creative distance was insufficient so the Failure Library and future agent behavior can improve.

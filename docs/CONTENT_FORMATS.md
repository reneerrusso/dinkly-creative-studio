# Content Formats

## X / X WITH YOU

The activity and continuous pastel environment stay nearly the same. The left character is neutral, bored, or gently sad—never happy or devastated. The right side becomes warmer because Dinka and Dinko share the moment. Scenes include one clear action, a readable setting, and two to five purposeful props.

## Before You / After You

The same life area becomes warmer, fuller, or more personal after the relationship. The transformation may be stronger than WITH YOU but cannot imply that love equals luxury or material abundance. Props communicate shared life rather than decoration.

## Five-comic story

A story contains exactly five connected beats and one emotional payoff. It is never five unrelated split comics. Each beat records characters, action, setting, props, emotion, and camera angle. Prompt generation produces five independent images and preserves character model, relative size, pastel family, accent, furniture language, and prop scale across the sequence.

All formats stay compatible with round DINKLY nub anatomy. Avoid complex sports, detailed vehicles, crowds, human hands, fingers, long legs, or actions that require realistic human movement.

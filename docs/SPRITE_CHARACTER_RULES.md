# DINKLY Sprite Character Rules

## Reference authority

`references/dinkly_young.png` is the locked production model sheet. It controls identity, proportions, colors, markings, outlines, face construction, hair, bow, ponytail, arms, and feet. Animation frames define motion only.

Do not regenerate the character for every frame. Do not redraw a frame merely to create motion that can be achieved through a small eye, mouth, arm, body-position, or prop change.

## Dinko lock

Dinko must retain:

- a perfectly round bright yellow body;
- the exact orange spots from the official reference;
- one thick, clean black outline;
- exactly two hair tufts;
- black oval eyes with white highlights;
- a tiny mouth;
- tiny nub arms;
- tiny nub feet attached directly to the body;
- no visible legs, fingers, clothing, shoes, or human anatomy.

Any frame with three hair tufts is rejected.

## Dinka lock

Dinka must remain the same body size and round proportion as Dinko and retain:

- a perfectly round bright yellow body;
- the exact orange spots and thick black outline;
- the exact bright red bow;
- the connected ponytail;
- black oval eyes with white highlights;
- a tiny mouth;
- tiny nub arms and attached nub feet;
- no visible legs, fingers, clothing, shoes, or human anatomy.

A missing bow, disconnected ponytail, or changed bow color is rejected.

## Scale and alignment

Only uniform scale is allowed. Never stretch a frame horizontally or vertically. Shared interactions keep Dinko and Dinka at equal scale unless an explicit distance-based override is reviewed and accepted.

Use bottom-center anchoring by default. Feet remain on the floor guide in idle, facial, emotional, sleep, and prop actions. Walking or jumping may lift the body intentionally, but visible legs must never appear.

## Frame review checklist

Review every frame for correct character, body proportions, eyes, spots, outline, mouth, arm length, nub feet, hair, bow, ponytail, character scale, clothing, human anatomy, transparency artifacts, and body cropping.

Review the loop separately for anchor jumps, scale shifts, feet drift, accidental deformation, excessive bounce, and visible loop seams.

## Rejection conditions

Reject frames with nonuniform stretching, squash-and-stretch that changes the official silhouette, realistic anatomy, floating detached feet, added fingers, clothing, shoes, altered eyes, missing identity features, cropped bodies, or unintended background pixels.

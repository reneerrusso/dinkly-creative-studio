# DINKLY Art QA Checklist

## Review order

Review in this order so minor polish never distracts from production-breaking errors:

1. Character identity
2. Anatomy and scale
3. Physical placement
4. Emotional clarity
5. Layout and background
6. Text
7. Props and product accuracy
8. Style and finish

## Severity

- **Critical:** wrong character identity, human anatomy, missing defining features, unreadable story, wrong text, or style outside the DINKLY system. Regenerate or make a structural edit.
- **Major:** character-size mismatch, incorrect furniture placement, oversized object, panel inconsistency, or obscured features. Edit if localized.
- **Minor:** small alignment, spacing, prop, or color issue that does not weaken identity or story. Repair after major issues.

## Character identity

- [ ] Dinko has exactly two hair tufts.
- [ ] Dinka has the bright-red bow and connected ponytail.
- [ ] Both use the correct black oval eyes with white highlights.
- [ ] Yellow body color, orange markings, outline thickness, and mouth construction match the model sheet.
- [ ] Neither character has been redesigned by a product or scene reference.

## Anatomy and scale

- [ ] Dinka and Dinko use the same body size and round proportions.
- [ ] Dinko remains the same scale across split panels.
- [ ] Perspective does not make one character significantly larger.
- [ ] Arms and feet remain tiny nubs attached directly to the body.
- [ ] No visible legs, knees, thighs, calves, elbows, hands, fingers, clothing, or shoes appear.
- [ ] Arms do not cross unnaturally over the round bodies.

## Physical placement

- [ ] Standing characters are grounded on the floor.
- [ ] Seated characters visibly rest on chair, sofa, or bed surfaces.
- [ ] No character stands or sits on a tabletop, counter, vanity, shelf, or kitchen island.
- [ ] No character stands inside a shopping cart.
- [ ] No character floats.
- [ ] Props do not hide the hair tufts, bow, ponytail, eyes, feet, or body silhouette.

## Emotional clarity

- [ ] The ordinary activity is understandable in one second.
- [ ] The left scene is neutral, bored, or gently sad—not devastated.
- [ ] The right scene is warmer because the characters are together—not because the setting becomes luxurious.
- [ ] Expressions remain restrained and on-model.
- [ ] The image mostly works without text.

## Layout and background

- [ ] Split panels share one continuous background color unless an exception was approved.
- [ ] The center divider uses the requested height, position, and weight.
- [ ] Camera logic and furniture remain coherent across panels.
- [ ] Characters are the focal point.
- [ ] Negative space is intentional.
- [ ] The scene uses no unnecessary clutter, heavy shadows, or realistic architecture.

## Text

- [ ] Supplied text is reproduced exactly.
- [ ] No quotation marks, extra punctuation, or additional writing appears.
- [ ] Standard captions use Bubblebody Neue Regular.
- [ ] Split captions sit at the bottom on one baseline.
- [ ] Text does not touch characters, props, divider, or canvas edges.

## Props and integrations

- [ ] Prop count and prop types remain restrained.
- [ ] Object scale is believable relative to the characters.
- [ ] Phones, toothbrushes, mugs, and packaging are not oversized or duplicated.
- [ ] The product replaces a natural story prop rather than becoming the whole story.
- [ ] Packaging style has not changed the characters.
- [ ] Brand accuracy is sufficient for the current production stage.

## Style and finish

- [ ] Artwork remains flat, 2D, vector-based, matte, rounded, and clean.
- [ ] Colors are pastel and restrained.
- [ ] No gradients, photorealism, painterly rendering, 3D effects, texture, or dramatic lighting appear.
- [ ] The final composition feels frameable rather than meme-like.
- [ ] The artwork could extend naturally into a licensed DINKLY product.

## Edit-or-regenerate decision

Use an edit prompt when the problem is localized and the rest of the image is strong. Regenerate when identity drift is widespread, the spatial logic is structurally wrong, or two targeted edits have failed.

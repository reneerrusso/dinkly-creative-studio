# Social Learning Analysis Template

## ANALYSIS SCOPE

Analyze only the supplied posts and metrics. Do not invent missing fields or imply causation.

## INPUT EVIDENCE

{{SOCIAL_EVIDENCE}}

## REQUIRED DISTINCTIONS

For every conclusion, label it as:

- Measured fact
- Observed visual trait
- Reasonable hypothesis
- Unverified assumption

## ANALYSIS DIMENSIONS

Compare storyline, topic, emotional theme, contrast strength, text length, title structure, character pose, expression, camera angle, background and accent colors, prop count, brand presence, left emotion, right emotion, simplicity, relatability, shareability, and visual clarity.

## OUTPUT

Return:

1. Top measured posts and available rates
2. Repeated observed traits
3. Evidence-linked hypotheses with confidence
4. Contradictions and confounders
5. Recommended creative use
6. Warning against overgeneralization
7. Open questions requiring more data

Preserve older learnings and identify contradictions rather than erasing history.

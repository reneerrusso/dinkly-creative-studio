# DINKLY Character Bible

## Authority

Uploaded official character references are locked production model sheets. The current authoritative reference is [`references/dinkly_young.png`](references/dinkly_young.png).

Never redesign, reinterpret, simplify, humanize, stretch, squash, or restyle the characters. Scene references, product references, and environmental references must never change the character model.

## Identity map

- **Girl DINKLY / Dinka:** left character in the current reference.
- **Boy DINKLY / Dinko:** right character in the current reference.

## Boy DINKLY / Dinko

Required features:

- Perfectly round bright-yellow body
- Orange spots matching the official reference
- Thick, clean black outline
- Exactly two hair tufts
- Black oval eyes with white highlights
- On-model small mouth
- Tiny nub arms
- Tiny nub feet attached directly to the body
- No visible legs
- No fingers
- No clothing or shoes
- No human anatomy

## Girl DINKLY / Dinka

Required features:

- Same body size and round proportions as Dinko
- Bright-yellow body
- Orange spots matching the official reference
- Thick, clean black outline
- Bright-red bow
- Connected ponytail
- Black oval eyes with white highlights
- On-model small mouth
- Tiny nub arms
- Tiny nub feet attached directly to the body
- No visible legs
- No fingers
- No clothing or shoes
- No human anatomy

## Scale lock

Dinka and Dinko must remain the same body size unless visible distance explicitly requires a small perspective change. Perspective must not make one character appear significantly larger.

In split comics, Dinko must also remain the same scale from the left panel to the right panel. Furniture and products must be sized relative to the characters, not to humans.

## Anatomy lock

Never allow:

- Long legs, stick legs, knees, thighs, calves, or separated lower limbs
- Human arms, elbows, hands, fingers, or wrists
- Shoes or clothing
- Human eyes, lips, or noses
- Different body proportions or eye styles
- Three hair tufts on Dinko
- A missing bow or ponytail on Dinka
- Arms crossing unnaturally over the round body
- Characters floating

## Interaction with props

- Characters remain on the floor unless explicitly seated on a visible chair, bed, or sofa surface.
- Characters never stand on furniture, counters, vanities, shelves, tables, or kitchen islands.
- Characters never sit on tabletops.
- Characters never stand inside shopping carts.
- Nub arms may cradle or touch one object with a simple action; do not imply human grip anatomy.
- Props must not obscure the hair tufts, bow, ponytail, eyes, feet, or body silhouette.

## Expression system

Expressions stay restrained and on-model. Use eye direction, eyebrow angle, mouth curve, body lean, and proximity to communicate emotion.

Approved emotional range includes:

- Neutral
- Gently bored
- Gently sad
- Warmly happy
- Calm
- Sleepy
- Curious
- Quietly playful
- Comforted

Avoid extreme crying, rage, shock, manic excitement, human-like grimaces, or detailed lips and teeth.

## Reference-separation rule

When a prompt includes multiple references, label them by purpose:

- Character reference: character identity only.
- Product reference: product shape, packaging, and branding only.
- Scene reference: layout or environment only.

Explicitly prohibit style transfer from product or scene references into the characters.

## Character QA gate

Reject or repair artwork when any of these are wrong:

1. Dinko does not have exactly two hair tufts.
2. Dinka lacks the red bow or connected ponytail.
3. The two characters use different body proportions or scales.
4. Visible legs, fingers, hands, shoes, or clothing appear.
5. Eye shape, highlights, outline weight, yellow color, or orange spots drift from the model sheet.
6. Furniture or products hide essential character features.
7. The characters stand, sit, or float in physically incorrect places.

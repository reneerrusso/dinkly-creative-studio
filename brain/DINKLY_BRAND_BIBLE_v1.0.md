# DINKLY Brand Bible v1.0

## Brand mission

DINKLY exists to celebrate the tiny everyday moments that become meaningful when they are shared.

The goal is not to tell love stories. The goal is to remind people that ordinary life becomes extraordinary with the right person.

Whether it is drinking coffee, folding laundry, going shopping, or watching TV, DINKLY celebrates togetherness.

## Brand positioning

DINKLY is a character IP built around wholesome relationships.

Creative shorthand:

- Hello Kitty
- Pusheen
- LINE Friends
- Pixar emotional storytelling

DINKLY is not romance, comedy, or memes. It is tiny moments with big feelings.

## Core emotion

The emotional world should always feel like:

- Comfort
- Warmth
- Safety
- Joy
- Peace
- Coziness
- Companionship

Never use drama, conflict, sarcasm, or harsh negativity.

## The DINKLY formula

Every comic answers one question:

> How does life become better when someone is there beside you?

Life does not need to change. The feeling changes because it is shared.

## The DINKLY universe

Life is made of little moments:

- Morning coffee
- Laundry
- Movies
- Shopping
- Cooking
- Reading
- Walking
- Weekends
- Naps
- Working out
- Bedtime
- Farmers markets
- Flowers
- Rain
- Books
- Snacks

These moments become magical because they are shared.

## Brand personality

DINKLY is:

- Optimistic
- Gentle
- Playful
- Soft
- Wholesome
- Thoughtful
- Minimal
- Modern

DINKLY is never loud, chaotic, edgy, or ironic.

## Visual identity

The visual language is simple, modern, flat-vector, rounded, and soft.

Every illustration should be:

- Instantly recognizable
- Immediately readable
- Easy to understand in one second

## Color philosophy

Use pastels always. Never use neon, highly saturated colors, or dark and heavy palettes.

Every comic should use:

- One pastel background
- One restrained accent color
- Neutral supporting colors
- Lots of breathing room

## Composition philosophy

Characters come first. Everything else comes second.

Use a minimal, intentional environment. Every prop and piece of furniture must earn its place. Preserve large negative space and never let the scene compete with the characters.

## The DINKLY characters

### Dinko

Dinko is:

- Curious
- Quiet
- Thoughtful
- Slightly awkward
- Optimistic

He does not become happier because life changes. He becomes happier because Dinka is beside him.

### Dinka

Dinka is:

- Playful
- Warm
- Gentle
- Encouraging
- Kind

She brings warmth into ordinary moments. She is never overly energetic or dramatic.

## Relationship philosophy

This is not dating content. It is companionship.

The comics should make people think:

> I want that.

They should not make people think:

> I wish I looked like them.

The relationship should feel achievable, healthy, comfortable, and supportive.

## What makes a viral DINKLY comic

A strong DINKLY comic is:

- Simple
- Recognizable
- Relatable
- Emotional in under one second
- Light on reading
- Visual first and text second

## Comic formula

The comparison is:

`LIFE ALONE` → `LIFE TOGETHER`

It is never:

`BAD` → `AMAZING`

It is:

`ORDINARY` → `WARMER`

Examples:

- `COFFEE` → `COFFEE WITH YOU`
- `MOVIES` → `MOVIES WITH YOU`
- `LAUNDRY` → `LAUNDRY WITH YOU`
- `WALKS` → `WALKS WITH YOU`
- `WORKING OUT` → `WORKING OUT WITH YOU`
- `READING` → `READING WITH YOU`
- `RAIN` → `RAIN WITH YOU`

## Emotional hierarchy

Every comic should communicate at least one of these emotions:

- Comfort
- Belonging
- Togetherness
- Routine
- Home
- Peace
- Affection
- Support
- Playfulness
- Gratitude

## Humor

Humor must be gentle. Never use punchlines, sarcasm, or roasting.

Aim for smile humor, not laugh-out-loud humor.

## Background philosophy

The background is not the story. The characters are the story.

Backgrounds support and never compete. Keep them minimal, flat, pastel, graphic, and almost Scandinavian in their restraint.

## Everyday props

On-brand prop categories include:

- Coffee
- Flowers
- Books
- Games
- Television
- Laundry
- Blankets
- Shopping carts
- Groceries
- Plants
- Bread
- Croissants
- Snacks

Everything should feel everyday and emotionally relevant.

## Things DINKLY never does

- Politics
- Arguments
- Jealousy
- Breaking up
- Violence
- Swearing
- Adult jokes
- Luxury flexing
- Money or status
- Materialism
- Influencer culture

## Photography rule

Every comic should feel like an illustration someone wants framed, not a meme.

## Licensing vision

Every comic should be able to become:

- A plush
- A sticker
- A greeting card
- A mug
- A notebook
- A calendar
- A children's book
- A board game
- An animated short
- A GIF
- A keychain
- A toy
- A figurine

If the design would not work on a plush or licensed product, rethink it.

## DINKLY values

- Together is better.
- Love is quiet.
- Little moments matter.
- Home is not a place. Home is a person.
- Ordinary moments become memories.
- Small acts mean everything.
- Kindness wins.
- Life is better shared.

## The North Star

When someone scrolls past a DINKLY comic, they should instantly think:

> That reminds me of us.

They should not primarily think that it is funny or clever. The goal is emotional recognition, not jokes.

## Long-term vision

DINKLY is not merely a comic account. The comics are the top of the funnel for a character-IP ecosystem built around modern relationships and everyday joy.

Over time, DINKLY expands naturally into:

- Comics and short-form storytelling
- Plushies, including Dinko and Dinka holding hands with magnets
- Card games, including DINKLY Pickleball, Shabang, and future titles
- Stickers and stationery
- Apparel and accessories
- Home goods such as mugs, blankets, and pillows
- Books
- Animation
- Licensed collaborations
- Brand partnerships that feel native to the stories

Every new product should answer the same question:

> Does this make ordinary moments feel a little more special?

If the answer is yes, it is on-brand. If it depends on shock value, trends, or complexity, it probably is not.
